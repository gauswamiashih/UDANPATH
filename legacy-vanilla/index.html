Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can final year students apply?

A: Yes, final year students are eligible for most exams like GATE, UPSC, and SSC CGL, provided they can produce their final degree certificates during verification.

Q: Is there any limit on attempts?

A: UPSC Civil Services has an attempt limit (6 for General, 9 for OBC, unlimited for SC/ST till age limit). GATE and SSC CGL have no attempt limits.
